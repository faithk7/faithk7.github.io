Interest

Running

I enjoy jogging in my spare time. Started running as an attempt to lose weight in high school, but soon the feeling of euphoria after a long run and my growing ability to control pace and rhythm gradually got me interested in this activity. Finished my first half marathon at Charlottesville Marathon in 2019 and now run about 4 miles per day to keep the habit of running.

Anime

A big fan of anime. Started watching anime since high school and fell in love with this world with boundless imagination and creativity immediately. It poses a big influence on me and is also the reason I get deeply interested in Japanese culture.

Favorite anime for now: Samurai Champloo, as its vision of life as a series of adventures, resilient attitudes toward challenges, contemporary artistic styles with the historical setting, and the ambient soundtrack captivate me.
