Info

Chinese Name: 瞿锴 (QuKai) | English Name: Kai I was Born in Hangzhou, but grew up in Beijing Speak Chinese (native), English (fluent) and Japanese (moderate).

Interest

Running

I enjoy jogging in my spare time. I started running as an attempt to lose weight in high school, but soon the feeling of euphoria after a long run and my growing ability to control pace and rhythm gradually got me interested in this activity. Finished my first half marathon at Charlottesville Marathon in 2019 and now run about 4 miles per day to keep the habit of running.

Music

For most of the time listen to J-pop. Favorite singer: Aimer. Her voice is powerful and comforting. I remembered the feeling of lonliness but also the joy of inner peace when listening to Polaris by Aimer, walking along in the campus at night.

Anime

A big fan of anime. Started watching anime since high school and fell in love with this world with boundless imagination and creativity immediately. It poses a big influence on me and is also the reason I get deeply interested in Japanese culture. Favorite anime for now: Samurai Champloo, as its vision of life as a series of adventures, resilient attitudes toward challenges, contemporary artistic styles with the historical setting, and the ambient soundtrack captivate me.

Reading

Enjoy reading all kinds of books. Nowadays read by genre. Started by reading the detective novels, and Edgar Allen Poe and Edogawa Ranpo are my favorite authors in this genre. Then switched to all categories. By reading many books I realized that the minds of humans are limitless, while we are bound by our bodies and actions. One of the favorite novels: From the new world. “Imagination changes everything.” And reading opens up the world of imagination.
